### Reported (not yet quotable as law)

- **No statute requires patient notice before a physician closes**; the duty is
  Medical Board guidance (written notice, at least 15 days of emergency
  treatment and prescriptions) plus the rule against abandonment. The Board's
  old "Closing Your Medical Practice" page returns 404; the guidance is on its
  Practice Information page (mbc.ca.gov).
- **HIPAA imposes no closure-notice duty and no medical-record retention
  period** (45 CFR 160/164; 164.530(j)'s six years covers compliance
  paperwork). HHS, 67 FR 53190 (2002): the sale rule "would not affect a
  covered entity's other legal or ethical obligation to notify individuals."
- **On a sale, records may pass to a buyer that is or will become a covered
  entity without patient authorization** — 45 CFR 164.501 ("health care
  operations" (6)(iv)), 164.506(c)(1), 164.502(a)(5)(ii)(B)(2)(iv).
- **A records custodian needs a business associate agreement** — 45 CFR
  160.103, 164.504(e).
- **H&S §123145 (the vendors' "seven years on closure") covers licensed
  facilities**, not a physician's or dentist's own office (H&S §1206(a)
  exempts those from clinic licensing).
- **No retention period was found for a dentist's private-office records.**
- **Medicare** 42 CFR 424.516(f): 7 years, for orders, certifications,
  referrals, prescriptions and payment requests only. **Medi-Cal** W&I
  §14124.1: 10 years.
- **DEA** 21 CFR 1301.52: registration ends when practice stops; notify
  promptly; surrender by DEA Form 104 or signed writing; dispose of stock under
  1317.05; on transfer to a successor, 14 days' notice and a joint inventory.
- **Medical practices cannot be sold to non-physicians** (B&P §2400, §2052(b);
  Corp. Code §13401.5). **Dental**: B&P §1625(e), §1626. **Optometry**: 16 CCR
  §1548; optical companies barred (B&P §655).
- **Fictitious-name permits do not transfer to a buyer**, dental (B&P §1701.5)
  or optometry (16 CCR §1518; a buyer may use the seller's name up to 2 years
  if the sale includes the active records).
- Do **not** cite 16 CCR §1549 or §1550 (optometry): repealed 2026-05-19,
  though the Board's 2024 law book still prints them.
