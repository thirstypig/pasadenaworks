## Broker compensation (Transition Planning, §4)

Queried 2026-09-21. All three are broker or broker-adjacent firms, so they are
evidence of how brokers describe their own fees, not independent surveys.
Cite the range, not a single figure.

- Dental Transitions, "Dental Practice Broker Commission Rates Explained" —
  success-based commission, typically 6–12% of transaction value, paid by the
  seller at closing.
  https://dentaltransitions.com/articles/dental-practice-broker-commission-rates/
- Dental Practice Reporter, "A Guide To What It Costs To Sell A Dental
  Practice" — 8–15% range; practices under ~$400K a year typically 10–12%.
  https://dentalpracticereporter.com/a-guide-to-what-it-costs-to-sell-a-dental-practice/
- Practice Transitions Group, "Understanding Broker Fees When Selling Your
  Practice."
  https://practicetransitionsgroup.com/blog/how-do-brokers-get-paid/
